Methodology & sources
Annual generation from capacity Annual generation (kWh) = Capacity (kWp) × 3.5 sun peak hours/day × 365 days. Standard planning assumption for Malaysia.
Grid Emission Factor Peninsular Malaysia 0.740 kgCO₂e/kWh, Sabah 0.539 kgCO₂e/kWh, Sarawak 0.199 kgCO₂e/kWh — Suruhanjaya Tenaga (Energy Commission Malaysia), Grid Emission Factor 2022–2024.
Emissions avoided Avoided emissions (tCO₂e) = Generation (kWh) × Grid Emission Factor (tCO₂e/kWh).
Tree equivalence Trees = Avoided emissions ÷ 0.04 tCO₂e per tree per year, sourced from MGTC's LCOS.
Car equivalence Cars = Avoided emissions ÷ 4.71 tCO₂e per car per year, derived from Peninsular Malaysia's Annual Average Vehicle Kilometres Travelled of 28,184 km and DEFRA 2025 passenger car emission factors.
Homes powered Homes = Annual generation (kWh) ÷ 4,140 kWh, the average annual electricity consumption of a household.
Lightbulbs powered Lightbulbs = Annual generation (kWh) ÷ 116.8 kWh, based on a 40W bulb running 8 hours a day for a year.
Football field equivalence Football fields = (Trees × 9 m²) ÷ 7,140 m². Assumes standard reforestation spacing of 3m × 3m (1,111 trees/hectare) and a FIFA-standard pitch area of 7,140 m².
